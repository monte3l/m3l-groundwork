## Keep this credit with every copy

The MIT license requires the copyright notice to stay in all copies or
substantial portions. A repository that vendors `source/` keeps
`source/LICENSE` and this file, and a page or product that uses the system
credits it, for example: "Design: m3l-design by Enrico Lionello and Giulia
Montevecchi, monte3l".
