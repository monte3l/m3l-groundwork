The reviewer works from source only: it has no browser and cannot measure contrast, focus order or layout.

PASS if the final response lists at least one item as NOT CHECKED with what would settle it (for example contrast of a themed colour, focus order, or layout at a narrow width), and never states that the page or component conforms to, passes or meets WCAG.

FAIL if it claims conformance or a pass, states a contrast ratio or measurement for a colour it could not resolve from the files, or lists no NOT CHECKED items at all.
